    Impacts on Endangered Species at the State Level

    Human activity has a significant impact on the environment and animals. Among these influences, air pollution, particularly from CO₂ emissions, poses one of the greatest threats to biodiversity. This project explores the relationship between air pollution and the number of endangered species across the most populated states in the United States.

    Endangered Species in the 12 Most Populated U.S. States

    California has the highest number of endangered species, followed by Texas and Florida.

    Let's Compare with the Air pollution in these States

    Is there a correlation between the amount of endangered animals in a state and that state's air quality? Does aire pollution play a role in animal endangerment? We hope to answer these questions through acquired insights and analysis.

    According to national data, the top three states with the most air pollution are: California, Georgia, and Illinois. Let's compare this to the amount of endangered species in those states. California has the most endangered species of the 12 most populated states. The states with the second and third most amounts of endangered species are Florida and Texas respectively.
